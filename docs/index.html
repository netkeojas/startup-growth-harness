how it works

Three calm steps. No screen in sight.

Learning a language feels like playing with a friend, and then it is time to put the toy down.

1

Pick a language

Choose English, French, German, Italian, or Spanish. Nuzzle starts at beginner level and grows with your child.

2

Play and talk

Short sessions of about 10 to 15 minutes: riddles, word games, and little stories. Nuzzle asks, hints, and cheers, so your child works out the answer.

3

Celebrate, then rest

Every session ends with a song and a soft glow, then Nuzzle says goodnight. No streaks, no pressure to keep going.
