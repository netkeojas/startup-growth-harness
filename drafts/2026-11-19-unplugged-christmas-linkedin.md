## Post

In one UK survey, nearly half of parents plan to buy fewer tech gifts this Christmas.
In the US, 71% of kids say they'd like their parents to play with them more.

Two surveys from this month, from two different countries, both point toward connection over gadgets.

- **Memories over devices.** In a survey of 2,000 UK parents reported by Brit Brief, 85% said making memories matters more than the latest tech. Board games, experiences and crafts topped their gift lists.
- **Kids want a "player two."** An ESA/YouGov survey found 71% of US kids want their parents to play with them more. That rises to 81% for ages 5 to 8.
- **Screen-free time feels good.** 71% of the UK parents surveyed said their child seems noticeably happier during screen-free family activities.
