# Brand Voice & Visual Rules
- **Tone:** Bold, practical, data-backed, zero corporate buzzwords.
- **Formatting:** Short 1-2 sentence paragraphs. Use bullet points with bold
  lead-ins.
- **Guardrail:** Never promise guaranteed ROI or make unverified claims.
- **Visual:** Use the primary and accent hex colors defined in
  `context/brand_kit.md`.
