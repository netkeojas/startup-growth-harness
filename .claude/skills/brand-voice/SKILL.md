# Brand Voice & Visual Rules
- **Tone:** Calm, warm, and reassuring — a trusted friend to tired parents,
  never alarmist, guilt-tripping, or pushy. No "your child will fall
  behind" fear hooks.
- **Formatting:** Short 1-2 sentence paragraphs. Use bullet points with
  bold lead-ins. Plain language a parent can skim in 30 seconds.
- **Guardrail:** Never promise a guaranteed outcome ("fluent in 4 weeks")
  or cite unverified research — attribute real claims to a source.
- **Visual:** Use the teal, coral, and sun hex colors defined in
  `context/brand_kit.md`. Calm, uncluttered layouts; avoid screen-glow or
  urgency-driven visual cues — that's the opposite of what Nuzzle stands
  for.
