# Startup: Nuzzle
- **Pitch:** A soft toy companion that teaches children ages 6–10 five
  languages — English, French, German, Italian, and Spanish — through
  games, riddles, and stories. Fully on-device and screen-free: nothing a
  child says ever leaves the toy, no internet required.
- **Target Customer (ICP):** Parents of children aged 6–10 who worry about
  screen time and data privacy, and want a calm, pressure-free alternative
  to language-learning apps.
- **Brand Promise:** Calm by design — 10–15 minute sessions, no streaks, no
  pressure, a CEFR-aligned syllabus, guided hints instead of answers, and
  every session ends with a song and a goodnight.
- **Brand Colors:**
  - Primary Teal `#0B5D68` (Deep Teal `#083F47` for dark/contrast use)
  - Accent Coral `#FF7D5C` (Soft Coral `#FFD9CE`)
  - Accent Sun `#FFCF4D` (Soft Sun `#FFE9A9`)
  - Background `#F4F8F6`, Ink/Text `#0F2A2E`, Muted `#4F6468`,
    Line `#D5E3E0`, White `#FFFFFF`
- **Website:** https://nuzzle-waitlist.vercel.app
